ARC-CLK | Financial & News Edition
ARC-CLK is a high-precision, architectural digital clock designed for professionals who need real-time market data and global news at a glance. Built with a minimalist aesthetic, it transforms your browser or device into a dedicated financial dashboard.

🚀 Key Features
Architectural Clock: Clean, high-contrast display of time, day, and date.

Live Market Ticker: Real-time tracking of Brent Oil, Gold, BTC, and major indices.

Triple-Stream News: Dedicated tickers for Hot News (Global), Local News, and Global Headlines.

PWA Ready: Install the application directly on your device for a native-app experience.

Adaptive Mode: Seamlessly switch between light and dark modes optimized for different lighting environments.

📲 Installation Guide (PWA)
You can install ARC-CLK as a standalone app on your computer or mobile device for quicker access and a full-screen experience without browser toolbars.

🖥️ For PC / Mac (Chrome or Edge)
Open ARC-CLK in your browser.

In the Address Bar, look for the Install icon (a computer with an arrow or a '+' sign).

Click Install.

The app will now appear in your Applications folder or Start Menu and can be pinned to your taskbar/dock.

📱 For iOS (iPhone / iPad)
Open the website in Safari.

Tap the Share button (the square with an upward arrow) in the bottom toolbar.

Scroll down and select Add to Home Screen.

Tap Add. The ARC-CLK icon will now appear on your home screen.

🤖 For Android
Open the website in Chrome.

Tap the three dots (Menu) in the top right corner.

Select Install App or Add to Home screen.

Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm.

🛠️ Built With
HTML5 & CSS3: For a responsive, "Wall Street" inspired layout.

Vanilla JavaScript: Lightweight and fast ticker updates and clock synchronization.

PWA Technology: Service workers and manifest integration for offline-ready installation.
